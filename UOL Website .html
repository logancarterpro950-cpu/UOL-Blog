About the University of Layyah

Posted by UL Blog Team | Category: University

The University of Layyah is a public sector university located in Layyah, a district in the southern part of Punjab, Pakistan. For many years students from this area had to travel to big cities to get a university degree. UL was started to change that, so that young people could study close to their homes and families.

Why UL was started

Layyah and the areas around it have a lot of talented students, but not enough chances for higher education. A university in the city means lower travel and living costs for families, and it also brings new jobs, new ideas and more activity to the whole region.

What students can study

The university offers programs in different fields. Some of the areas students can choose from are:

  • Computer Science and Information Technology
  • Management and Commerce
  • Basic and Applied Sciences
  • Arts, Languages and Social Sciences
Education is not just about getting a degree, it is about building a better future for yourself and your community.

Our identity

If you look at the UL logo you will notice a green leaf branch, a bold orange UL and a network of connected dots. The leaves show our roots in the green, farming land of Layyah, and the connected dots show science, technology and the way students and ideas come together.

Student Life at UL

Posted by UL Blog Team | Category: Campus Life

Life at a university is not only about sitting in classrooms. At UL, students get a chance to learn outside the books too. From the first week on campus, you meet new people from different towns and backgrounds, and that itself is a big part of the experience.

Learning inside the classroom

Teachers at UL try to make classes useful and not just theoretical. Students do assignments, presentations and group projects, which helps them improve their communication and teamwork. These are the same skills that employers look for after graduation.

Activities outside the classroom

There is always something going on during the semester. Students can take part in:

  • Sports events and friendly matches
  • Seminars and guest talks
  • Society and club activities
  • Competitions in speech, quiz and other skills

Tips for new students

If you are just starting at UL, do not worry if everything feels new at the beginning. Go to your classes regularly, ask questions when you do not understand something, and join at least one society. Make friends with seniors, because they can guide you about courses, exams and how things work on campus. Most importantly, enjoy your time, because these years pass very quickly.
